Open EncoreSettings…Quit

No window open, Encore still playing. Press the ⏯ key (or click it here) and playback pauses and resumes from the menu bar. Safari isn't involved.

Why the media key always works

Encore registers its own Now Playing session with macOS and keeps it alive while paused. The player lives in a hidden web view that never closes, so ⏯ resumes without bringing any window forward.

Audio mode

The video is hidden and YouTube streams at 144p to save data, while the sound keeps full quality. Colours are pulled from each video's thumbnail, so every track gets its own palette.

Five visualisers

Halo, Spectrum, Waves, Nebula and Ridgeline run off a live frequency analysis of the audio. They are drawn natively at 60 fps in the Mac app, in the menu bar too.

Your real YouTube

You sign in once and get your home feed, subscriptions, history and playlists. Clicking any video hands it to the player, so browsing never interrupts what's playing.
